# Surge Pricing v0.3.0: Sound and kinder controls

**Play:** https://ahab94.github.io/surge-pricing/

## Sound
Everything is synthesized in code, so nothing is licensed.
- **City music:** a theme per city: a musette waltz in Paris, swing in New York, bossa in Rio, a tarantella in Rome, Arabic pop in Dubai, ska in London, city pop in Tokyo, a 9/8 dance in Istanbul, pentatonic mallets in Bangkok, synthwave in Singapore. Press M to mute the music.
- **Driving:** the engine follows your speed, the tyres squeal in drifts, sirens grow louder as they approach, and the rain cities have rain.
- **Events:** crashes, honks, close-call whooshes, jumps, splashes, ka-chings, review chimes, fines, the arrest fanfare and the motorcade gong.
- **Passengers** mumble in their own voice while they talk.

In a browser, sound starts after your first click or tap.

## Controls
- **Walls:** grazing a wall slides you along it instead of bouncing you back. Only a real head-on hit is a crash.
- **Steering:** tighter at speed, and a hard turn scrubs speed so corners are makeable. Keyboard steering eases in.
- **Driving aids:** lane assist when you let go of the wheel, wall avoidance at corners, and an unstick nudge if you're pinned against a wall or a car.
- **Traffic** that's stuck nose-to-nose with you pulls over to let you pass.
- **Stops:** bigger pickup and drop-off zones, and a slow roll counts.

In testing, a new-player bot hits walls about half as often.
