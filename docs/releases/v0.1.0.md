# Surge Pricing v0.1.0

The first release: an arcade rideshare game where **your driver rating is your health bar**. You drive for Yoink, a parody app. Pick up passengers, drive like a maniac for tips, and beat the shift clock. Drop below the rating floor and you're deactivated.

**Play in your browser (phones too):** https://ahab94.github.io/surge-pricing/

## What's in it
- **7 shifts**, each adding one thing: traffic and close calls, Livestreamers and ramps, Nana, Hustle Bros and drifting, moving surge zones that pay double, rain.
- **4 passenger types who want opposite things.** Each has their own reviews and speech bubbles ("MY HIP!", "CHAT SAW THAT!", "That's a write-off.").
- **Special traffic:**
  - police chases: ram the getaway car for a Citizen's Arrest;
  - ambulances that everyone pulls over for;
  - a garbage truck;
  - a VIP motorcade you must NOT touch.
- **An angled 3D city** with a canal you can jump if you're going flat out.
- **Touch controls on phones and tablets** (floating stick, Brake, Drift), in portrait or landscape.

No sound in the game yet. That's next.

## Install
- **Browser:** the link above.
- **macOS / Linux:** `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/surge-pricing/main/install.sh | bash`
- **Windows (PowerShell):** `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/surge-pricing/main/install.ps1 | iex`
