# Surge Pricing v0.2.0: World Tour

Ten cities, one shift each, and the passengers talk like locals.

**Play in your browser (phones too):** https://ahab94.github.io/surge-pricing/

## What's new
- **The World Tour.** Paris, New York, Rio, Rome, Dubai, London, Tokyo, Istanbul, Bangkok and Singapore. Each city has:
  - its own map, look and landmarks;
  - its own local traffic: yellow cabs, red double-deckers, tuk-tuks, scooters, supercars;
  - one new thing to learn.
- **Driving on the left** in London, Tokyo, Bangkok and Singapore.
- **The night shift** in Singapore: lit windows and headlights.
- **Local passengers.** Nana is Nonna in Rome, Nan in London, Obaachan in Tokyo and Ah Ma in Singapore. The Hustle Bro is a Crypto Bro in Dubai and a Salaryman in Tokyo. Every city has its own lines, for example:
  - "MY LASAGNA!" (Rome)
  - "Hey, I'm RIDIN' here!" (New York)
  - "Wah lao!" (Singapore)
  - "Liquidity event! LITERALLY!" (Dubai)
- **Talking passengers.** Speech bubbles for crashes, jumps, close calls, splashes and dawdling.
- **Special traffic:**
  - police chases (ram the getaway car for a citizen's arrest);
  - ambulances everyone pulls over for;
  - garbage trucks;
  - a VIP motorcade you must not touch.
- **Smoother rendering** on high refresh rate screens, and traffic that turns corners.

Landmarks are generic shapes, and every business in the game is a parody.

Still no sound in the game. That's next.

## Install
- **Browser:** the link above.
- **macOS / Linux:** `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/surge-pricing/main/install.sh | bash`
- **Windows (PowerShell):** `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/surge-pricing/main/install.ps1 | iex`
- Or download a zip below. The installers check each download against SHA256SUMS. The builds are not code-signed.
